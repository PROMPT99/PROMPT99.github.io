01 / About the project

Built out of curiosity.

Photosphere is my personal project for exploring Earth and the solar system through interactive tools. It brings together my interests in astronomy, design, and web development.

The idea

I wanted to make a place where people could explore scientific information visually, from finding research locations on Earth to looking around our solar system.

Earth Searcher

Earth Searcher uses an interactive 3D globe to help users find universities, observatories, radio telescopes, and research centers. Search and filtering tools make it easier to explore places and learn about them.

Planetary Tool

The Planetary Tool lets users explore the solar system, select planets and moons, view their orbits, and compare distances. It is a way to get a better sense of the objects in our solar system and the space between them.

My work

I created the site's visual identity and interface, brought the tools together using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, CesiumJS, and Three.js, and worked on features, performance, and usability. Developing Photosphere has involved experimenting, troubleshooting, and refining the experience as I learn.

Still developing

Photosphere is an ongoing project. I am continuing to improve the tools, fix issues, and explore new ideas for making Earth and space easier to discover.
